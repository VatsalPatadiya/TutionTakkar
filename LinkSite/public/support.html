Contact us

You can also send an idea or a bug report right from the game: Settings › Send us an idea (signed-in players).

Common questions

How do I play?

Pull back on your pen to aim, let go to flick it. Knock the other pen off the desk while yours stays on. First to win 3 rounds wins the match.

How do I play with a friend?

Open Play Online, make a private room and share the room code or link. Signed-in players can also add friends and invite them straight from the room.

I don't get friend notifications

Allow notifications for Tuition Takkar in your phone's settings and make sure you're signed in. On iPhone, Settings in the game shows whether friends can reach your phone.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

Sign in with Google or Apple and your account, avatar, name, wins and friends come with you. Guest progress stays on the phone it was played on.

How do I delete my account?

See Deleting your data in our privacy policy, or email us with the subject "Tuition Takkar account deletion".
