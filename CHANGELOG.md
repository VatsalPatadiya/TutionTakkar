# Tuition Takkar · Changelog

## v1.0 · first store release (2 Nov 2026)

Builds: Android 1.0 (version code 1, Play Store AAB) · iPhone 1.0 (build 5) · web at tuitiontakkar.web.app/play

**Gameplay**
- Flick-to-shoot pen fight on a school desk; first to win 3 rounds (best of 5)
- Batch Ladder of rivals (win their pens), Practice vs computer, Pass & Play
- 2, 3 and 4-player "last pen standing" desks

**Online**
- Quick Match with real players (computer classmates fill in after a wait), private rooms with a code or link
- 28 desks from Primary School to the Champions' Palace, with entry fees, prize pots and levels
- Friends: player codes, requests, presence, room invites; drawn emoji reactions in matches
- Leaderboard, public profile, badges and stats

**Accounts**
- Sign in with Google (Android and iPhone) and Sign in with Apple (iPhone), or play as a guest
- The account owns the avatar and name: they come back on any phone

**Make it yours**
- Avatar maker (hair, eyes, glasses, hats…) and an easy 3-step pen maker

**Notifications**
- Friend requests and room invites as phone notifications (Android through Firebase, iPhone through Apple)
- 7 PM reminders

**Feedback**
- "Send us an idea" for signed-in players; owner page /admin/ideas with player profiles and replies that notify the player

**Web**
- Browser version with page links (/play/quick, rooms, results), privacy, support and 404 pages
